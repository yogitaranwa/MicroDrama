# Harry + Draconia: Yule Ball S1 - E02 "The Locked Rehearsal" (detailed draft v1)

Target: story-led, about 100-110 seconds inside the 60-180s bounds; the cliffhanger sets the cut. 16 shots. 15 dialogue lines. Continues directly from E01's final line. Vertical 9:16, cinematic photoreal, same wardrobe and Great Hall as E01.

Emotional spine: adrenaline drains into suspicion. Harry stops being grateful and starts demanding answers; Draconia's bravado cracks only when she is alone with the truth. The dance floor itself becomes the antagonist, so fear is physical, not discussed.

## Per-line detailing (voice direction, v3 tags in brackets only where verified)

1. HARRY "Warn me about what?" - emotion: cornered anger over fear; delivery: low, slow, each word placed. Emphasis: "what". Pause before: one beat while the door seals. Reaction: Draconia won't meet his eyes.
2. DRACONIA "Not here. Not while it's listening." - [whispers] rushed; she scans the ceiling. Emphasis: "listening". Reaction: instruments rising confirms it.
3. CURSE "One. Two. Three." - emotionless, patient, wrong. Slow count, off the actual waltz tempo. (Narrator-class voice, not Harry or Draconia.)
4. HARRY "Tell me those are just reflections." - [nervous] but fighting to sound level. Emphasis: "just". Reaction: he already knows.
5. DRACONIA "They're what we're supposed to be." - quiet, disgusted. Pause after: she refuses the curtsy. Emphasis: "supposed".
6. HARRY "It copied my footing. It doesn't copy temper." - [angry] mid-parry, breathless. Delivery: fast, through exertion.
7. DRACONIA "It dances better. I cheat better." - [laughs] delighted, [rushed]. Emphasis: "cheat". Reaction: first real fun she's had.
8. HARRY "It's not attacking us. It's casting us." - dread arriving as understanding. Slow. Emphasis: "casting".
9. DRACONIA "This is the founding trial... finishes the waltz." - controlled recitation cracking at "my family's". Pause before the last sentence. Emphasis: "only".
10. HARRY "Then we finish it. And afterward, you tell me everything." - formal, hard. Emphasis: "everything". Reaction: he means it as a condition, not comfort.
11. DRACONIA "Every right step feeds it... It's a lesson, Potter." - bitter amusement, breathless in motion. Emphasis: "lesson".
12. DRACONIA "Wrong steps, it keeps a piece of you. Stop testing it." - [urgent], genuine fear for him for the first time. Emphasis: "keeps".
13. DRACONIA "There's a forfeit's step... I just didn't want to pay it." - quiet, ashamed. Pause before "I just didn't". Emphasis: "didn't want".
14. HARRY "Don't you dare pay it alone." - [urgent] [angry], the loudest he's been. Emphasis: "alone". Reaction: she expected him to agree; this lands.
15. CURSE "Rehearsal two. Bring your honesty." - pleased, patient. Emphasis: "honesty". Cut to black on the last word.

## Voice/performance notes

- Harry: E01's control breaks into audible anger twice (line 10 implied, line 14 explicit); keep the voice low until line 14, so the crack means something.
- Draconia: bright deflection until shot 10; her register drops when family enters it. Line 13 is the episode's emotional floor: slow, ashamed, no wit.
- Curse voice: neutral measured narrator-class delivery; do not perform villainy, the wrongness is in the patience.
- Brisk overall pace, but do not rush lines 9, 13, 14 - those are the beats the episode exists for.
