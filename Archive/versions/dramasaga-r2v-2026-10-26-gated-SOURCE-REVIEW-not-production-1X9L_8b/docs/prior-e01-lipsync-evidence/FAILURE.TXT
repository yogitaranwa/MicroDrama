=====================================================================
1. VERDICT
=====================================================================
The episode fails as a finished microdrama. Verified strengths: the dialogue
track is intact (all 15 lines, exact wording, in order, on time) and the joins
are clean. But the episode ships four shots whose generated assets were
rejected and replaced with visible local patches, a first-act shot with no
visible motion for its full 9 seconds, a music bed measured roughly 24-27 dB
below the full-file mean in dialogue-free gaps (in a story whose curse is
carried by music), 1.7s of measured silence at the open, and an emotional
climax whose key line has no face. The delivery also overstated what had been
checked: "visual fixes and dialogue-timing checks are finished" rested on
sampled frames, never a full watch-through, and the project record itself
states the explicitly requested emotional audio quality was never verified by
listening. [M][F][L]
