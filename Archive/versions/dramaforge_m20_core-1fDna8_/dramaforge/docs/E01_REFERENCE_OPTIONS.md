# E01 design options from the reference reel (for the owner's call, no script change made)

Source: public reel instagram.com/reel/DfAJgYVjP3K (@katechon_studio), photoreal AI microdrama, 45 s, 8.9k likes, analysed by the owner's team. Facts below are as relayed.

## Pattern in the reference
- 45 s story, about 20 shots, 3 locations, one dialogue line per shot, 2-4 s clips.
- About 12 of 20 shots are close-ups, one epic wide, one full-body hero shot.
- 3-4 prop insert shots (book closing, clock, tankards) mark beats. They need no face consistency and hide AI clip seams.
- Location identity comes from colour temperature alone (cool library, warm corridor, blown-out doorway, warm pub).
- One outfit per character per episode, which makes face consistency read as the same person.
- Background AI humans are dark, soft-focus and under 2 s.
- Audio is one continuous score plus close-mic whispered dialogue as one original sound. No trending audio, no text overlays. The hook lives in the caption.
- Writing: a micro-conflict every 3-5 s. Comedy by reversal plus a callback that pays off twice.

## Fit with our pipeline
- 2-4 s single-line clips match the LatentSync path (about 4 min of T4 per 7 s of video, so about 12 min for a 20-shot episode at 3 s each).
- Our E01 has 8 locations and 90-120 s. The reference has 3 locations and 45 s.

## Options (owner decides)
A. Keep E01 as is. Add 3-4 prop inserts and a colour-temperature tag per location. Lowest effort, keeps the 90-120 s length.
B. Merge E01 to 3-4 locations, keep 90-120 s, more coverage per location (more close-ups, fewer wides). Needs a script and shot-list rewrite.
C. Rebuild E01 as a 45-60 s cut on the reference pattern (3 locations, about 20 shots). Shortest, cheapest to render, biggest rewrite. Conflicts with the current 90-120 s canon length.
Cross-cutting adds, cheap under any option: one outfit per character per episode, background humans dark and under 2 s, one continuous score under close-mic dialogue, no text overlays, and a hook caption.

## Constraints still in force
Dialogue stays full sentences with emotional depth, 1.4x pace (1.5x ceiling), a hard cliffhanger, and no fillers. Fewer shots means each line carries more.
