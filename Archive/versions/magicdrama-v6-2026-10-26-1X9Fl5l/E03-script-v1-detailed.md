# Harry + Draconia: Yule Ball S1 - E03 "Brooms Over the Black Lake" (detailed draft v1)

Target: story-led, about 100-110 seconds inside the 60-180s bounds; the cliffhanger sets the cut. 16 shots. 14 dialogue lines. Continues from E02's ice stairwell. Vertical 9:16, cinematic photoreal. New location: frozen Black Lake at night; wardrobe unchanged under traveling cloaks.

Emotional spine: escape becomes pursuit. Draconia saves Harry at real cost and, cornered by her own action, admits she knew part of the curse before the ball. Trust breaks open mid-flight - the argument happens at speed, not standing still.

## Per-line detailing (voice direction, v3 tags in brackets only where verified)

1. DRACONIA "Whatever you find down here, we leave now." - [urgent], low, eyes on the stair. Emphasis: "now". Reaction: the crawling ice punctuates her.
2. HARRY "Your family's handwriting. Before the ball. Talk." - controlled, cold. Pause between sentences, no rush. Emphasis: "before". Reaction: he already suspects; the page is confirmation.
3. DRACONIA "Fly first. Hate me at altitude." - [rushed], deflection with real fear under it. Emphasis: "altitude". Reaction: she can't hold his eyes.
4. HARRY "It's the same count! It's following the waltz!" - alarmed realization mid-weave, breathless, fast. Emphasis: "same".
5. DRACONIA "Then stop flying on the beat!" - [urgent] [rushed], half-laughing at the absurdity. Emphasis: "flying".
6. HARRY "Little help!" - [nervous] underplayed: Harry's version of panic is brevity. Reaction: he refuses to look down.
7. DRACONIA "I have you. I have you." - quiet, fierce, repeated like a promise to herself. Emphasis: second "have". Reaction: the mark flares; she hides the pain badly.
8. HARRY "You knew. Before the ball. Some part of this." - low, flat, worse than shouting. Pause after "You knew." Emphasis: "knew".
9. DRACONIA "The warning spell was mine... I didn't know it would mark you." - fast confession, [rushed], then slows hard on "I didn't know". Emphasis: "mark". Pause before the last clause.
10. HARRY "You used me as the other half of a pair you picked?" - hurt dressed as accusation. Emphasis: "picked". Reaction: he wants to be wrong.
11. DRACONIA "I picked the person who wouldn't run. That wasn't strategy. That was the mistake." - quiet, no defense left. Emphasis: "mistake". Pause before the last sentence. This is the episode floor.
12. HARRY "The lake lets go at the forest. That's not rescue, that's directions." - tactical brain back online, grim. Emphasis: "directions".
13. DRACONIA "Second dance. Midnight. It's inviting us in." - exhausted awe. Emphasis: "inviting".
14. HARRY "Then we go in honest. All of it, tonight." - resolved, not warm. Emphasis: "all". Reaction: cut to black before she answers.

## Voice/performance notes

- The argument (shots 10-13) happens at flight speed: lines are short because they're flying, but the emotional content is full. Do not slow the pace; let the weight live in the pauses within lines 9-11.
- Draconia's confession (line 9) must sound torn out of her, not composed. Harry's line 10 is the angriest and the quietest he has been in the season.
- Wind/count ambience carries the curse; keep dialogue-forward mixing as before.
