- A: Corridor outside the Great Hall - warm amber candlelight, 2700K

- C: Seventh-floor corridor, Room of Requirement wall - blown-out white-gold doorway glow, overexposed highlights
- D: Astronomy Tower stairwell - cold silver moonlight, deep blue shadows

- Draconia Malfoy: emerald ball gown with black travelling cloak, braid
- Harry Potter: charcoal dress robes, loosened collar
- Pansy Parkinson: dark green velvet dress
- Professor McGonagall: emerald-trimmed black robes, tartan shawl
- Argus Filch: grey greatcoat, lantern
