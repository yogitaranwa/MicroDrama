**Title:** The Confession in the Tower
**Format:** Microdrama (approx. 90 seconds, 8 scenes)
**Target Audience:** Young Adults

### SCENE 1
**INT. GRAND STAIRCASE - NIGHT**
The YULE BALL echoes behind a stone wall. HARRY POTTER (17) adjusts his robes, looking ruffled. DA MALFOY (17, Draconia) stands beside him, tapping her foot, looking furious.

HARRY
You’re pacing a hole in the floor, Draconia.

DA
I’m not pacing. I’m vibrating with rage. You humiliated me in front of half the school, Potter. That “mistaken identity” bit was cruel.

HARRY
It wasn’t intentional. You were wearing my jacket.

DA
(Scoffs)
Because my own house couldn’t find a date who didn’t stare at me like I’d poisoned the mead!

A low, WHEEZING sound echoes from the shadows. ARGUS FILCH peeks around the corner, lantern in hand, looking suspicious.

FILCH
What’s all this then? Noise in the corridors after curfew? You two shouldn’t be out here. Professors like order.

Harry steps forward, calming.

HARRY
We’re just... leaving. We’re leaving, Filch. No trouble.

Filch narrows his eyes, grunts, and shuffles away, wheezing.

FILCH
Tch. Teenagers. No respect for the rules.

As Filch rounds the corner, Harry and Draconia exchange a look—a flicker of mutual annoyance, but also reluctant amusement.

### SCENE 2
**INT. HALLWAY NEAR ASTRONOMY TOWER - NIGHT**
They walk briskly. The portraits watch them. Draconia stops, crossing her arms.

DA
I’m not staying in there. Pansy is already triangulating who I danced with to analyze my "strategic marital alliances."

HARRY
(Murmurs)
You have time for that later. Much later.

DA
I don’t. I have to go. Now.

She turns to leave, but Harry gently catches her elbow. Not a romantic grip—just a grounding touch.

HARRY
Wait. Where are you going?

DA
(Snaps)
None of your business.

HARRY
(Smiling, dry)
If I’m the “reserved one,” you’re the one with the secret agenda.

Draconia freezes. Her expression shifts from anger to a vulnerable, quick-flashing guilt.

DA
...I promised I’d meet someone. In the library. Someone my father... wouldn’t approve of.

HARRY
(Eyebrow raise)
A secret admirer?

DA
(Whips head around)
Hardly. Someone I’m not allowed to be seen with. If my father knew I was skulking off with a Gryffindor...

She trails off. The tension isn't just about the Ball anymore. Harry studies her face, noticing the small lines of worry she usually hides behind a smirk.

HARRY
...You’re meeting a friend. A Slytherin friend?

DA
(Nods, sharp)
Someone who needs help. Someone the "Golden Boy" wouldn’t be caught dead associating with. Unless...

She steps closer, voice dropping to a whisper.

DA
...Unless you’re also chasing a ghost. I heard you asking Filch about the cabinet. The one with the locked drawers.

Harry pauses. He looks at her—really looks at her.

HARRY
Someone’s been stealing from Filch. Insect legs, a few dried bats... trivial stuff. But it’s been happening all term. I was trying to find out why.

Draconia’s eyes narrow, then soften. She looks away, kicking a pebble.

DA
My... acquaintance. They needed the ingredients. For a potion. Not dark. Just... complicated.

HARRY
(A soft smile)
So we’re both liars. I’m chasing thefts; you’re breaking curfew for a secret meeting.

DA
(Laughs, a short, sharp sound)
Potter, you’re nosy.

HARRY
Weasley says I have "saving-the-world" syndrome. You, I’m guessing, just have "family duty" syndrome.
