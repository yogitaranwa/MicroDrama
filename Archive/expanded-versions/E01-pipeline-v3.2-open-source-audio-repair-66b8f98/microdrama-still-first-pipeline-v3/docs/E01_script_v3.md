# Harry + Draconia: Yule Ball E01 v3.2

Working title: **The Waltz That Attacked First**

Target length: 90-100 seconds. Twelve shots. Fifteen spoken lines. The scene should end when the reveal lands; no padding.

## Editorial rule

Dialogue should sound like people under pressure. Use contractions, visible clues and short complete thoughts. No lore lecture, no quip stacking, no line that exists only to fill time.

## Character bible, locked for E01

- Harry: messy black hair, round glasses, white dress shirt, plain black tie, dark trousers and a plain black formal robe with a thin red inner lining. Slim wooden wand in his right hand. No waistcoat, no tuxedo jacket, no sword, no blade, no metal cuffs.
- Draconia: platinum blonde hair in the same braided crown/updo as the accepted reference. Emerald green embroidered sleeveless Yule gown with sheer dark green shoulder drapes. No long loose side braid, no necklace change, no costume change.
- Palette and mood: candle gold, cool blue shadows, restrained expressions, shallow depth of field. Danger first, banter second.

## Scene causality

- Harry is the target: the black spark and arrows track him, not the room at random.
- They must keep moving: a frozen dancer and glowing floor marks show what happens when someone stops.
- Draconia knows the danger: she recognizes the Malfoy ward mark, but she does not know the full plan.
- The masked figure is established visually before it matters: it appears in a window reflection, then on the floor.
- The armor is visible before anyone names it.
- Breaking the waltz breaks the curse's expected pattern, so the curse binds the two people who broke it.
- The final reveal is new information: Draconia sent Harry a warning, and someone intercepted it.

## Voice direction

- Preserve accepted voice identities. Change delivery, not identity.
- Draconia: urgent and controlled at first, defensive when Harry challenges her, genuinely afraid at the marks, honest on the final line.
- Harry: calm, observant and dry, but not jokey. His suspicion lands only after the marks.
- Leave breathing room between emotional beats. Do not make every line fast.
