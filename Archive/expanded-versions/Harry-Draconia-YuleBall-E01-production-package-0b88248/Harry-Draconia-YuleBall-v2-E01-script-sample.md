# Harry + Draconia: Yule Ball S1 - revised sample v2

## Season direction

Original alternate Yule Ball story. Cinematic photoreal, audio-first H3, ten episodes. Natural episode length can go up to 180 seconds; do not pad to 180 seconds. Episode lengths are story-driven within 1-3 minutes. The sample below is 95 seconds.

The new version starts in motion, keeps a visual set piece or reversal every few seconds, and uses dialogue as action. Harry is reserved, observant and tactical. Draconia is impulsive, theatrical and braver than she admits. No haunted-house debt exposition and no copied reel beats.

## Ten-episode arc v2

1. **The Waltz That Attacked First** - A curse turns the first dance into a battlefield and marks Harry and Draconia as paired witnesses.
2. **The Locked Rehearsal** - The ball refuses to release them until they recreate the cursed waltz perfectly while its reflections attack.
3. **Brooms Over the Black Lake** - A stolen clue leads to a night broom chase over the frozen lake; Draconia saves Harry and admits she knew part of the curse.
4. **The Upside-Down Stair** - Gravity reverses in the hidden stair; Harry solves the pattern while Draconia duels the armored dancers.
5. **The Warning Spell** - Harry discovers Draconia cast the warning that saved him weeks before the ball.
6. **The Pretend Betrayal** - They stage a public break to lure the masked caster into the open.
7. **The Gaunt Champion** - The caster reveals himself as an old Slytherin trial given form and demands proof of loyalty.
8. **Her Choice** - Draconia refuses a family command, shields Harry, and loses the protection of her house crest.
9. **The Mirror Confession** - Harry sees what Draconia risked for him; he chooses her in front of the school.
10. **Second Dance** - They break the trial by choosing the dance without being forced. The forest sends a final invitation: "Second dance. Midnight. Bring the heir of Slytherin."

## Voice/performance notes

- Harry: low, controlled, economical. He sounds calm because he is processing danger, not because he feels safe.
- Draconia: fast, bright, cutting, visibly thrilled by danger until the final line. Her fear lands only when she says she tried to warn him.
