# Harry Potter - The Uninvited Guest

Original fan scene; 56-second production edit. Four featured characters plus one dragon.

Warm afternoon. Students eat and talk in the Great Hall. Owls wheel beneath the enchanted ceiling.

HERMIONE: Do you ever stop eating?

RON: Usually when you start revising.

Harry smiles. Beside a newly delivered letter, an owl suddenly becomes still.
Other owls wheel toward the rafters.

A deep impact rolls through the stone. Goblets tremble. Conversations fall away.

RON: Please tell me that was a staircase.

Dust settles on Hermione's book. A crack runs up the entrance arch.

HERMIONE: Away from the entrance. Now.

A second impact. The entrance masonry crashes inward. Students shelter beneath tables.

At floor level, an enormous clawed foot lands in the broken stone.
The camera rises: a charcoal dragon forces its shoulders into the Hall, wings folded.
Smoke leaks from its nostrils. It turns toward the Gryffindor table.

Harry raises his wand.

HARRY: Stay behind me.

The dragon stops. Black robes emerge beside its foreleg. Voldemort steps through the dust.

He finds Harry in the crowd.

VOLDEMORT: There you are.

Extreme close-up of Voldemort's eyes. Orange light flickers in them.

Cut to black.
