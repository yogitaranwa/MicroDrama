# The Billionaire's Scullery Maid — Season 1 (5 × ~1 min)

Original characters, English dialogue, 16:9, burned-in captions.
Style: cinematic realistic drama (glossy vertical-drama look). Can be switched to anime before rendering.

## How your story is used

- **Every scene and every line of dialogue from your story is kept exactly as written** — same events, same order, same words. These shots are marked **[ORIGINAL]**.
- New material is only **added around** your story to make 5 episodes (marked **[ADDED]**): the childhood rescue flashback, Vivian (the stepmother) on screen, Chloe's first morning as assistant, the brother Leo, and an ending after your final scene.
- Nothing in your story was removed or rewritten. Some long lines are split across two shots so each one is spoken clearly; the words are unchanged.

## Production rules applied (from the earlier films)

| Problem we hit before | Rule in this script |
|---|---|
| Background kept changing between shots | Every location has one locked **environment plate**; every shot names its plate |
| Voice changed between shots | Every speaking character has one fixed **voice description**, reused word for word |
| Lip sync broke on multi-speaker takes | **One on-screen speaker per shot** |
| Model added mumbled extra words | Max ~14 words per 6 s shot, ~20 per 8 s shot; every shot checked with `intelligibility.py` |
| Story not understandable | Each episode opens on a **hook in the first 4 s** and ends on a **cliffhanger** + caption |
| Continuation felt wrong | Fixed wardrobe per episode; props tracked below |
| Expensive surprises | Shots are 4, 6 or 8 s only; render Ep 1 first, check, then Ep 2–5 |
