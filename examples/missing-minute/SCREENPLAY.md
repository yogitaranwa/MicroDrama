# Harry Potter: The Missing Minute

Original fan screenplay. Set during Harry's fifth year. Estimated duration: 4-5 minutes; record and time dialogue before locking the edit. Character appearances are original interpretations, not actor identity matches.

## Scene 1 - The voice (about 35 seconds)

INT. HOGWARTS CORRIDOR - NIGHT

Harry stops outside a disused classroom. His hand hovers above the latch.

LILY (O.S., ordinary, affectionate): Harry. You'll be late.

He almost answers. Hermione comes around the corner.

HERMIONE: What are you doing?

HARRY: You heard her?

Hermione looks at the door. No answer comes.

INT. DISUSED CLASSROOM - CONTINUOUS

A damaged stone Pensieve rests on a wooden table. A small coil of silver memory circles its basin. Beside it: a Ministry evidence tag.

INSERT - POTTER RESIDENCE. UNRECOVERED MEMORY.

HERMIONE: Harry. The date.

The tag is dated tomorrow.

Harry has already reached for the silver. Cut on contact.

## Scene 2 - Breakfast (about 35 seconds)

INT. POTTER KITCHEN - MORNING - MEMORY

A kettle begins to steam. Lily wipes porridge from a toddler's cheek. James laughs somewhere upstairs. Harry stands at the edge of this ordinary morning, unable to move closer.

The toddler bangs a spoon against his bowl. Lily catches it before it falls.

The image breaks. Harry is back beside the Pensieve.

HERMIONE: What did you see?

HARRY: Nothing.

He looks at her; it is a poor lie.

HARRY: They were having breakfast.

He reaches again. Hermione catches his wrist.

HERMIONE: Your watch.

Its second hand has stopped. The classroom clock has advanced seven minutes.

## Scene 3 - The impossible viewer (about 40 seconds)

Hermione examines the tag, staying beside Harry rather than turning away from him.

HERMIONE: Department of Mysteries. That doesn't explain how it got here.

HARRY: She looked at me.

HERMIONE: She couldn't have.

HARRY: She was looking at the door. I was standing there.

HERMIONE: What did she say?

He starts to answer, then stops.

HARRY: I don't remember.

HERMIONE: But you remember the room?

HARRY: Yellow curtains. A blue cup with a broken handle.

His hand lowers. On the wall, his shadow remains extended toward the bowl for one beat too long.

Hermione draws her wand. The shadow drops.

HERMIONE: Step away from it.

Harry does not.

## Scene 4 - The bargain (about 45 seconds)

Harry touches the memory before Hermione can stop him.

INT. POTTER KITCHEN - MEMORY

Lily notices the older boy standing in her kitchen. She approaches slowly.

LILY: You look like him.

Harry cannot speak.

LILY: Your father. When he thinks he's in trouble.

She lifts a hand toward his scar. Harry leans into the gesture.

INTERCUT - CLASSROOM

Silver curls around Harry's wrist. Hermione braces a hand against the table and pulls him back.

LILY: Someone's hurting you.

Harry tears his arm away from Hermione. The classroom door slams. She strikes it, catching herself with one hand.

Harry emerges from the memory.

HARRY: You could have broken it!

Hermione keeps her injured hand close to her body.

HERMIONE: You haven't asked me why I'm bleeding.

Harry sees the blood. His anger drains.

## Scene 5 - The price (about 45 seconds)

Hermione turns the Ministry tag over. Fine writing has appeared beneath its seal.

INSERT - A LIFE REMEMBERED MUST BE PAID FOR WITH A LIFE REMEMBERED.

HERMIONE: It isn't taking time. It's taking memories.

HARRY: I remember everything.

HERMIONE: Where did we meet?

HARRY: On the train.

HERMIONE: What was I looking for?

Harry waits for an answer that should come easily.

HERMIONE: Neville's toad.

HARRY: I knew that.

HERMIONE: Tell me about the troll.

He looks at her as if she has asked him about a stranger.

A kettle whistles on an empty desk. Cool moonlight begins changing to morning light.

## Scene 6 - The choice (about 55 seconds)

Lily stands on the other side of the Pensieve. She seems more solid than the classroom behind her.

HERMIONE: Harry. Break the bowl.

Harry raises his wand. Lily steps into its line.

LILY: You don't have to lose me again.

His wand trembles. He lowers it a fraction.

HARRY: What did you call me?

LILY: Harry.

HARRY: When I was little.

Lily hesitates.

Hermione's voice comes from behind him, quiet and frightened.

HERMIONE: I don't know either.

Harry turns toward her. He recognizes her face; the reason it matters is slipping away.

Lily offers her hand. Harry takes it. Silver closes over his palm.

He cannot aim at her face. Instead he turns the wand toward his own trapped hand, down into the bowl.

HARRY: Reducto.

CUT TO WHITE. The sound of stone breaking.

## Scene 7 - What remains (about 30 seconds)

The classroom is dark again. Hermione kneels beside Harry. His palm is burned. Broken stone lies around the table.

HARRY: Neville's toad.

Hermione lets out a breath she has been holding.

HERMIONE: Do you remember the kitchen?

Harry searches. He shakes his head.

HERMIONE: The memory might have started from something real.

A long silence.

HARRY: What did she sound like?

Hermione has no answer. She helps him stand.

At the doorway she extinguishes the lamps. They leave together.

In the empty classroom, among the broken pieces, a kettle begins to whistle.

CUT TO BLACK.

## Production requirements

- Preserve the relationship and emotional progression; do not replace missing actions with unrelated spectacle.
- Render clean frames without generated text. Add the evidence tag and inscription as tracked graphics.
- Produce Lily's memory separately. Composite silver, shadow delay, window light change and apparition as controlled effects.
- Record dialogue and obtain an actual listening review before timing or lip-sync renders. No synthetic placeholder is accepted dialogue.
- Keep readable faces in emotional lines. Offscreen voices must be intentional and supported by reactions.
- Reuse locked character references and wardrobe across all angles. The kitchen is a separate environment.
- Review each shot against its action and intended emotion before accepting it into the film.
- Do not generate the full film under the short-test budget.
