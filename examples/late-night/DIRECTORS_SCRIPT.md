# Working Late (directed)

*Mia told Tomas she was working late. At one in the morning she sneaks home in the dark and finds him waiting in the kitchen with her receipt.*

## Visual bible

- **Format:** 16:9, 24 fps, 35 mm lens equivalent, shallow depth of field, soft natural film grain, restrained naturalistic colour, no stylised grading, handheld-steady realism
- **Set:** a small city apartment at night: a short hallway runs from the front door to the kitchen doorway on the kitchen's west wall; in the kitchen a rectangular wooden table stands in the middle, a tall window onto city lights fills the east wall, a fridge covered in notes and magnets stands against the north wall, teal cabinets and a counter run along the south wall
- **Geography:** Mia enters from the hallway at the WEST doorway. The wall light switch is on the kitchen side of that doorframe, at hip height, to the right as she enters. Tomas sits on the EAST side of the table facing WEST toward the doorway, the tall window behind him. Dialogue coverage is over-the-shoulder along the line between them: the reveal is shot from just behind Mia looking east, the answers from just behind Tomas looking west. Keep the table, window, fridge and cabinets in the same places in every shot.
- **Lighting:** Before the switch the kitchen is dark: only cool blue city light through the east window and a faint streetlight glow on the floor. The overhead kitchen light is controlled only by the wall switch and turns on only when Mia presses it, with a small click. After that every shot has the same two sources: a warm overhead light (about 2700 K) and the cool window light behind Tomas, so faces are lit warm from above with a cool rim from the window.
- **Performance:** Naturalistic and restrained, like a film, not a sitcom: small micro-expressions, no grins, no wide-eyed shock, no big gestures. Mia is tired and guilty; Tomas is calm and hurt. Nobody raises their voice.
- **Wardrobe and props:** Mia: olive trench coat over a cream top, shoes in one hand. Tomas: grey crew-neck sweater, short dark beard. On the table in front of Tomas: a crumpled paper receipt and a cold mug of coffee. No cake.

## Shots

### 1. The Return (5 s)

**Intent:** Establish that she is sneaking home at one in the morning and that the kitchen at the end of the hall is dark.

**Camera:** medium-wide, starts at knee height behind her; slow dolly forward that rises from knee height to chest height as it follows her down the hallway; 35 mm lens.
**Light:** Dark. Cool blue glow from the far end of the hall on the floor; her face is barely lit.
**Begins with:** Inside the dark apartment, just past the front door: Mia has her back to us, her hand still on the door she has just closed; her shoes dangle from her other hand; the hallway ahead is dark with a faint cool glow at its far end.

| Time | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 0.0-1.5 s | Mia eases the front door the last inch shut with her free hand, holds perfectly still and listens. |
| 1.5-3.5 s | She tiptoes down the dark hallway toward the kitchen doorway where the faint blue glow spills on the floorboards; the camera follows and slowly rises. |
| 3.5-5.0 s | She stops at the kitchen doorway, one hand sliding along the wall beside the doorframe, feeling for the light switch. |

**Performance:** Careful, tired, guilty; she is trying to make no sound at all.
**Sound:** quiet apartment at night: refrigerator hum and faint distant traffic; the front door latch clicking softly shut; floorboards creaking under bare careful steps; fabric of the coat brushing the wall
**Ends with:** Mia stands at the kitchen doorway seen from behind and slightly from the side, her free hand flat on the wall beside the doorframe feeling for the switch, not yet pressed; the kitchen beyond is dark with cool window light.

### 2. The Switch (3 s)

**Intent:** Show why the light comes on: she turns it on, and the light is what reveals the room.

**Camera:** close-up, low, hip height; tilts up from her hand on the switch to her face; 35 mm lens.
**Light:** Dark until her finger presses the switch; then the warm overhead light comes on at once, spilling across her.
**Begins with:** Exactly where the previous shot ended: Mia at the kitchen doorway, her hand on the wall beside the doorframe feeling for the switch. *(continues straight from the previous shot's last frame)*

| Time | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 0.0-1.0 s | Her fingers find the switch plate and press it: a small click, and the overhead kitchen light comes on. |
| 1.0-2.4 s | The camera tilts up from her hand to her face as the warm light reaches her; the relief of being home turns into stillness as her eyes find something across the room. |
| 2.4-3.0 s | She freezes, breath held, shoes still dangling from her hand, gaze fixed across the kitchen. |

**Performance:** A flicker of relief, then complete stillness. No gasp, no wide eyes.
**Sound:** quiet apartment at night: refrigerator hum; a small, distinct wall light-switch click; the faint buzz of an overhead light warming up
**Ends with:** Mia stands frozen in the kitchen doorway, lit by the warm overhead light, shoes dangling from her hand, her eyes fixed on the table across the room.

### 3. The Reveal (4 s)

**Intent:** Reveal that Tomas has been sitting in the dark kitchen the whole time, calm and waiting.

**Camera:** medium over-the-shoulder, eye level; slow dolly in toward Tomas; 35 mm lens.
**Light:** The warm overhead light is already on from the switch in the previous shot; cool window light behind Tomas gives him a thin rim.
**Begins with:** From just behind Mia's left shoulder (her shoulder and hair soft and out of focus in the left foreground), looking east across the lit kitchen: Tomas sits at the far side of the table in front of the tall window, hands folded, a crumpled receipt and a cold mug in front of him, already looking at her.

| Time | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 0.0-1.2 s | Hold on Tomas, perfectly still, eyes already on Mia. The camera begins its slow push in. |
| 1.2-3.2 s | He lets out a slow breath and leans a little back in his chair, then speaks quietly. |
| 3.2-4.0 s | He keeps his level gaze on her. |

**Performance:** Calm, hurt, controlled. Almost no movement.
**Sound:** quiet apartment at night: refrigerator hum; a faint chair creak as he leans back
**Tomas** (quiet, dry, not sarcastic): "Working late?"
**Ends with:** Tomas has leaned slightly back in his chair, level gaze on Mia, receipt visible on the table in front of him.

### 4. The Answer (4 s)

**Intent:** Her side of the exchange: she is caught and tries to sound casual.

**Camera:** medium close over-the-shoulder, eye level; slow push in toward Mia; 35 mm lens.
**Light:** Warm overhead light on Mia's face; the doorway and dark hallway behind her.
**Begins with:** From just behind Tomas's right shoulder (his shoulder and dark hair soft and out of focus in the right foreground), looking west at Mia in the kitchen doorway, shoes in her hand, warm overhead light on her face.

| Time | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 0.0-1.3 s | Mia swallows, her eyes drop to the table for a moment and come back up. The camera pushes in slowly. |
| 1.3-3.2 s | She crouches an inch to set her shoes quietly on the floor, straightens, and speaks softly, trying to sound casual. |
| 3.2-4.0 s | She holds his gaze. |

**Performance:** Tired and guilty, small movements; a weak attempt at a casual tone.
**Sound:** quiet apartment at night: refrigerator hum; shoes being set down on the wooden floor
**Mia** (soft, tired, trying to sound casual): "Tomas... you're still up."
**Ends with:** Mia has set her shoes down on the floor beside her; her hands are empty and she looks at him.

### 5. The Receipt (4 s)

**Intent:** The hook: he already knows. He slides the receipt toward her and says the line that ends the lie.

**Camera:** medium close-up, slightly low, table height; very slow push in on Tomas's face; 35 mm lens.
**Light:** Warm overhead light on his face with the cool window rim behind.
**Begins with:** Medium close-up of Tomas at the table, receipt under his fingertips, level gaze toward Mia.

| Time | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 0.0-1.2 s | He looks down at the receipt under his fingertips. |
| 1.2-2.0 s | He slides it a few inches across the table toward her with two fingers, never taking his eyes off her. |
| 2.0-3.6 s | He says the line, flat and quiet. |
| 3.6-4.0 s | He holds his steady gaze. |

**Performance:** Flat, quiet, devastating. No anger.
**Sound:** quiet apartment at night: refrigerator hum; paper sliding across the wooden table
**Tomas** (flat, quiet, final): "Your office closed at six."
**Ends with:** Tomas holds his steady gaze; the receipt now sits near the edge of the table toward her.

**Hook title:** She said she was working late...  |  **End card:** What will Mia say?
